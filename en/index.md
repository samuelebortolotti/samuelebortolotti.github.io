# Welcome

I am Samuele Bortolotti, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Trento, working with the [Structured Machine Learning Group](https://sml.disi.unitn.it/) under the supervision of [Stefano Teso](https://stefanoteso.github.io/) and [Andrea Passerini](https://disi.unitn.it/~passerini/index.html).

I work on machine learning, trustworthy AI, and neuro-symbolic integration. I study how machine learning models learn from data, and where and why they fail — for example, *reasoning shortcuts*, where a model reaches the right answer for the wrong reasons. I want AI to support people and to be trustworthy, grounded in explicit, inspectable rules written in logic.

In spring 2026, I spent three months at the [APRIL Lab](https://april-tools.github.io/), working with [Antonio Vergari](https://nolovedeeplearning.com/) on constraint acquisition with Large Language Models.

I earned my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Computer Science, both cum laude, from the University of Trento in 2021 and 2023, respectively.

I am always open to collaboration or a chat, whether you are interested in my research, curious about what I do, or simply want to say hello. Feel free to reach out in English, Italian, or German. My German is still a work in progress, so please bear with me if something gets lost in translation :)
